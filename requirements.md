# AROGYA-MITRA Requirements

## Overview

AROGYA-MITRA is an AI-powered healthcare intelligence system that analyzes uploaded medical documents and provides treatment cost comparison, hospital recommendations, government scheme eligibility, and alternative treatment options to help patients receive effective and affordable healthcare.

---

## Problem Statement

In India, patients often lack transparency regarding treatment costs, hospital success rates, and affordable treatment options. Medical reports are complex, and patients do not know which hospital provides the best treatment at the lowest cost.

Additionally, many patients are unaware of government healthcare schemes like Ayushman Bharat, which provide free or subsidized treatment.

This results in financial burden, delayed treatment, and suboptimal healthcare decisions.

---

## Proposed Solution

AROGYA-MITRA allows users to upload medical documents such as prescriptions, lab reports, and discharge summaries.

The system uses AI to:

- Extract diagnosis and treatment information
- Predict treatment costs across hospitals
- Compare hospitals based on cost, reviews, and success rate
- Identify government scheme eligibility
- Suggest alternative treatments including Ayurveda and preventive care
- Recommend the best treatment option based on cost and effectiveness

---

## Objectives

- Improve transparency in healthcare costs
- Help patients choose affordable treatment options
- Reduce financial burden of medical treatment
- Improve access to government healthcare schemes
- Support preventive and alternative healthcare options

---

## Target Users

- Patients
- Rural populations
- Elderly individuals
- Low-income families
- Healthcare decision makers

---

## User Inputs

- Medical documents (PDF, images)
- Patient age
- Location
- Optional medical history

---

## Functional Requirements

The system must be able to:

- Accept medical document uploads
- Extract diagnosis using AI
- Predict treatment costs across hospitals
- Compare hospitals based on cost and success rate
- Identify eligible government healthcare schemes
- Recommend affordable treatment options
- Suggest alternative and preventive treatments
- Provide multilingual support

---

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Scalable cloud infrastructure
- Secure medical data handling
- Fast response time
- High availability
- Privacy protection

---

## Expected Outcomes

- Improved healthcare decision making
- Reduced treatment costs
- Increased access to affordable healthcare
- Improved healthcare transparency

---

## Impact

Supports national healthcare initiatives including:

- Ayushman Bharat
- Digital Health Mission
- Affordable Healthcare for All
